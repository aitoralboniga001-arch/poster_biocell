# Design rationale (v3)

**One cell, one story, no cuts.** The macrophage is the focal point. Everything around it is either a magnified view of a place in that cell or a consequence of what happens there. Nothing is a separate slide.

## Visual grammar
- **Two routes, two colours.**
  - The phagocytic route is vermilion.
  - The enzyme (biosynthetic) route is cobalt.
  - Both meet at a violet fusion site (4) and give the phagolysosome.
  - Soft colour ribbons under the organelles make the convergence readable from several metres away.
- **Magnifier lenses** are the only peripheral format, the convention used in journal figures. Each round inset has a coloured rim and a cone or dashed link back to its source in the cell:
  - alveolus;
  - receptor contact site;
  - fusion stages;
  - killing systems;
  - inflammation;
  - lung histology.
- **Step tags** (2.1 → 2.7, 3.1 → 3.6, 5.2 → 5.5) give the order. The section number in each heading matches the speaker:
  - 1 Aitor;
  - 2 Nerea;
  - 3 Urtzi;
  - 4 Eneritz;
  - 5 Nora;
  - 6 Aitor.
- **Arrows.** There are 6 arrow types, each with one meaning: phagocytic, enzyme route, fusion, recycling, pathological effect and inflammatory signal.

## Flow of the talk
1. **Alveolus lens** (top left). The small macrophage zooms into the large cell.
2. **Contact-site lens.** Receptors and opsonins, then the engulfment chain, then an arrow into 2.3 inside the cell.
3. **Inside the cell, top right.** The enzyme route from the ER to the lysosome.
4. **Fusion site ring 4.** Leads to three fusion lenses (tether → zip → pore), then to the killing lenses: NOX2 vs acid hydrolysis.
5. **Pathology column.** The asbestos fibre crosses out of the cell and IL-1β flows out to the inflammation lens. The step tags run down an ochre timeline into normal vs fibrotic lung.
6. **Take-home.** It receives both arrows: clearance (green, from killing) and persistence (ochre, from fibrosis).

## Typography (v4)
An editorial pairing of two variable fonts with true optical sizes. Both ship with Windows 11 and are embedded in the PDF.
- **Sitka (serif): the story.**
  - Title: Banner cut, with "destroys" set in true italic.
  - Section headings: Display cut.
  - Narrative paragraphs, captions and the take-home: Text cut.
  - Every italic on the poster is a real Sitka italic, never a slanted sans.
- **Segoe UI Variable (sans): the figure.** All labels inside the cell and the lenses.
  - Display cut for organelle names.
  - Text cut for notes.
  - Small cut for tags.
  - Bold is set as semibold, for a lighter, more refined colour on the page.
- **Hierarchy at a glance:** serif = what you read, sans = what you look at.
- **Details:**
  - small caps with wide letter-spacing for kickers and speaker names, in the section colour;
  - kerning and ligatures on, lining numerals;
  - figure captions in journal style: *a* **Tethering.**
- **Sizes:** title about 88 pt, headings about 30 pt, organelle names about 20 pt, body text about 17–18 pt.
- **Text amount:** about 650–840 visible words (about 1,490 before).

## Illustration style (v4)
- **One light model:** light from the top-left, everywhere.
  - Bodies have lit radial fills with a deeper far rim.
  - Soft contact shadows sit down-right.
  - A small specular highlight on the lit side of each body.
- **Membranes:** drawn as toned bilayers (two leaflets with a pale core), never as plain black outlines.
- **Organelles in detail:**
  - nucleus: double envelope, real pores, heterochromatin against the envelope;
  - Golgi: top-lit, with a cis→trans colour shift;
  - rough ER: two-tone ribosomes;
  - mitochondria: folded cristae;
  - silica crystal: faceted.
- **Magnifier lenses** are drawn as glass loupes: a bezel, an inner rim shadow, a glare highlight and a soft drop shadow. Zoom cones are gradient light beams.
- **No hard edges:**
  - every arrow is a tapered, gently curved brush stroke with a rounded head;
  - recycling dashes have round caps;
  - leader lines curve slightly;
  - tags are pills;
  - the earlier ruled timelines were removed: the numbered step tags carry the order instead.
- **Pure vector:** gradients only, no filters or bitmaps, so the print stays sharp at any size.
