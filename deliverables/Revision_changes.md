# Revision v3: what changed

## Composition
- **Header.** The dark navy banner, the speaker strip and the large arrow legend are gone. In their place: an open serif title, a subtitle, and the authors at the top right. Speaker names now appear only as small tags next to the section headings.
- **One visual language outside the cell.** Every detail outside the cell is a round **magnifier lens** linked by a zoom cone or dashed link to the spot it magnifies in the cell. This replaces the rectangular "slide" cards:
  - the alveolus (1);
  - the receptor contact site (2.1);
  - the three fusion stages (4);
  - the two killing systems;
  - inflammation;
  - normal vs fibrotic lung (5).
- **A continuous process.** Nothing on the page is a hard section break. Flow lines carry the story through the poster:
  - 1 → 2;
  - engulfment → the nascent phagosome in the cell;
  - fusion → killing;
  - an ochre timeline for 5.2 → 5.5 that ends in the take-home;
  - a green clearance arrow from killing into the take-home.
- **Pathology starts inside the cell.** The asbestos fibre leaves the macrophage and crosses into the pathology column. IL-1β leaves the cell towards the inflammation lens.
- **Conclusion.** The dark block is replaced by a light take-home that receives both outcomes: clearance and persistence.
- **Key.** The large molecule legend is replaced by one compact key: 6 arrow types and 6 symbols. Most molecules are now labelled where they act.

## Central macrophage
- **Asbestos.** The fibre is now held in an **open phagocytic cup**: pseudopods spread along it and the cup stays open (frustrated phagocytosis). It no longer pierces the membrane. NOX2, ROS and enzymes leak out of the open pocket.
- **Silica.** The injured phagolysosome moved next to its text. The tear faces NLRP3, and IL-1β leaves the cell.
- **NOX2** now appears on the nascent and early phagosomes (the burst starts early), not only on the phagolysosome.
- **Early endosomes** are shown as kiss-and-run exchange, not emptying.
- **Removed:** the antigen-presentation side route (it now gets one line in "Killing and digestion"), the microtubules (they were read as leader lines) and the black "maturation" arrow.
- **Filopodia** are redrawn as solid, actin-filled protrusions.
- **Step tags.** Sequential steps 2.1 → 2.6 (phagocytic) and 3.1 → 3.6 (enzyme route) carry the order. The key says which steps are sequential and which run in parallel.

## Final polish (after an independent check)
- **Killing and digestion** is now explicitly step **2.7**, Nerea's last step, magnified from 2.3–2.6. This means no section is left without a speaker.
- **Engulfment flow arrow** now ends at the phagocytic cup instead of running inside the cell.
- **Fusion badge 4** was moved so it no longer covers the fusing vesicles.
- A stray arrow floating over the Golgi was removed.
